## Design Specificity Verdict

### Verdict: Generic AI Template Slop Masquerading as High-End Luxury
The current interface is textbook AI boilerplate disguised under superficial token replacements ("Lagos", "Ikoyi", "Victoria Island"). Beneath the surface, the design borrows indiscriminately from generic SaaS landing pages and Dribbble trends, completely lacking the cultural gravity, architectural grandeur, and bespoke hospitality required for elite Nigerian event production.

### Core AI Slop Tropes Identified:
1. **The Typographic Monoculture (Poppins Everywhere)**: Using Google's `Poppins` for all elements (display, pull quotes, body, tags, numbers) and forcing "luxury" via `font-weight: 300` and aggressive negative letter-spacing (`-0.07em`) causes glyph collisions and feels like an unstyled wireframe. True luxury ateliers in West Africa command high-contrast editorial serifs (*Cormorant Garamond*, *Ogg*, *Canela*) anchored by disciplined grotesque sans-serifs.
2. **The "Hero Metric" Counter Cliché (`8+` / `500+` / `4` / `∞`)**: Flaunting `"4 Event Categories"` as an achievement and slapping an infinity symbol (`∞`) on `"Lagos & Beyond"` is pure SaaS marketing kitsch. Claiming `500+ Events Delivered Without a single hitch` breeds skepticism rather than reassurance.
3. **The Rotated macOS Browser Mockup**: Presenting bespoke couple websites inside a rotated tech browser window (`transform: rotate(1.4deg)`) with traffic light dots cheapens multimillion-Naira celebrations into a tech software commodity.
4. **Asymmetric Card Radii & Glassmorphic Glaze**: Arbitrary border radii (`64px 42px 88px 42px`, `42px 76px 44px 90px`) and universal `backdrop-filter: blur(10px–20px)` create visual noise without structural intent.
5. **Cultural & Contextual Disconnect**: Unsplash stock imagery depicts Caucasian couples in European open fields, western party balloons, and generic auditorium stages. Authentic Lagos luxury hallmarks (opulent floral canopies, banquet dining at The Monarch/Eko Hotel, bespoke Aso Ebi styling, live bands, and grand protocol) are missing.

### Deterministic Scan Findings:

- `bounce-easing` in `src/styles/global.css` (line 101): `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` applies an elastic bounce on button interactions, conflicting with high-end luxury decorum.
- Additional technical audit findings: 18 defects including synchronous layout thrashing (`el.offsetTop` inside scroll handler in `Navbar.jsx`), unobserved dynamic portfolio elements in `useScrollReveal`, missing WCAG focus rings in booking inputs, and severe text contrast failures on footer and process tips.

## Overall Impression
The color palette (warm charcoal, rosewater, champagne, and blush) provides a solid foundation, and the modular component architecture with portfolio modals shows good frontend hygiene. However, the site is currently suffocated by generic AI templates, broken contact conduits, and SaaS gimmicks that undermine client trust.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Dead-End Channels, Phantom Contacts & Bait-and-Switch CTA
- **Why it matters**: WhatsApp and Instagram are the primary sales channels for Nigerian event hosts. Linking to `wa.me/2348000000000`, blank `instagram.com`, and having "View Full Portfolio" bounce the user to `#contact` destroys brand legitimacy immediately.
- **Fix**: Connect authentic studio channels (`@toshevents` handles, real concierge WhatsApp link). Replace the "View Full Portfolio" anchor jump with an expandable in-page gallery or a "Download Lookbook (PDF)" modal.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Cultural & Visual Inauthenticity (Generic Stock Imagery & Copy)
- **Why it matters**: A premier Lagos atelier cannot sell luxury galas using Caucasian models in European meadows. High-net-worth clients require proof of mastery over local cultural aesthetics, grand floral architecture, and high-capacity venue coordination.
- **Fix**: Replace stock photos with authentic luxury celebration imagery (rich traditional attire, grand floral installations, grand ballroom banquets). Ground copy in authentic Lagos event production context.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable bolder`

### [P1] Typographic Monoculture & Geometric Sans Overuse
- **Why it matters**: Universal use of Poppins 300 weight with extreme negative letter spacing (`-0.07em`) causes glyph collisions and flattens all editorial hierarchy.
- **Fix**: Pair an exquisite, high-contrast luxury display serif (*Cormorant Garamond*, *Ogg*, or *Playfair Display*) for hero headlines, section titles, and pull quotes, with a clean grotesque sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Inter*) for body copy and UI controls.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

### [P2] Visual Gimmicks & SaaS Template Tropes
- **Why it matters**: The rotated fake browser window, chaotic asymmetric border radii, infinity stat counter (`∞`), and juvenile confetti explosion drag the studio down into generic tech-landing-page slop.
- **Fix**: Remove the rotated browser frame in favor of an elegant mobile concierge mockup. Standardize card radii to a disciplined architectural curve (e.g., 20–24px). Remove `canvas-confetti` in favor of a dignified confirmation card.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

### [P2] Layout Thrashing & Contrast Accessibility Failures
- **Why it matters**: `offsetTop` inside the scroll handler causes frame drops, `transition: width/padding-left` causes reflow jank, and muted text contrast fails WCAG AA standards (< 3.1:1 on footer tagline and process tips).
- **Fix**: Replace `offsetTop` with `IntersectionObserver`, animate GPU-accelerated `transform: scaleX()` instead of `width`, and adjust muted text colors to meet WCAG AA 4.5:1 minimums.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize`
